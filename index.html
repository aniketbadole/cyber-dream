About the Dream

The dream I can’t stop thinking about is to build technology that makes people safer, smarter and more independent. I’m currently studying cybersecurity, and the more I learn, the more I realise how much of our digital world is held together by trust — trust that our data is safe, our systems are protected, and ordinary people are not left vulnerable to threats they don’t understand. My goal is to build AI‑powered cybersecurity tools that make protection accessible, not just for big companies with huge budgets, but for students, small businesses, and everyday users too.

Vision & Goals

Cybersecurity shouldn’t be a luxury. I envision a future where intelligent tools proactively shield users from threats, teach them safer habits and empower them to take control of their digital lives. By harnessing advances in artificial intelligence and privacy‑preserving technologies, we can make the invisible defenses of the internet robust and accessible to all.

I have already started working on prototypes and side projects in security and AI, but to build systems that truly matter I need the room to focus. This platform showcases the beginning of that journey.

Why This Scholarship Matters

Studying abroad brings many pressures: tuition, rent, living costs and the constant need to balance survival with ambition. A scholarship would not just help financially; it would give me the breathing space to continue learning deeply, to build seriously and to take my ideas further. It’s not just about obtaining a degree — it’s about becoming someone who builds things that matter.
